About Springfield Financial

Empowering SMBs with world-class financial solutions since 2012

Our Story

Founded in 2012 by James Spencer, Springfield Financial Services Ltd was born from a single conviction: that SMBs in emerging markets deserve the same quality of capital support enjoyed by developed economies.

Our mission is to fuel sustainable business success—because when local businesses thrive, entire communities benefit. We deliver speed, simplicity, and tailored financing that matches your cash-flow cycles.
